Our Department

Creating Future-Ready Technology Professionals

The Computer Applications Department provides a dynamic academic environment where students build strong foundations in programming, software development, database technologies, web development and emerging areas of computing.

The department focuses on combining academic knowledge with practical experience through laboratory sessions, projects, workshops, seminars, competitions and collaborative learning activities.

Through TechConnect, students can stay informed about department events, activities, achievements and opportunities while becoming part of an active technology community.
